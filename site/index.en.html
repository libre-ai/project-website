Work and learn with AI

Projects that keep you in control of your sources, tools and decisions.

Recovered code is being integrated and tested locally. This catalogue does not list deployed services or published packages. The Travel project has data and checks, but no itinerary application yet.

Products

Libre AI Work Supervision

Keep track of work entrusted to AI agents without losing sight of what you asked for, what is blocked and what has actually been completed. This proposed product is for people coordinating work with agents who need a clear place to review progress and make decisions.

Libre AI Model Policy

Understand whether a proposed use of an AI model fits your organization's rules, and why. This product is intended for people choosing models or reviewing their use who need an explained decision rather than an unexplained yes or no.

Libre AI Practice Workbench

Learn through practice with AI while understanding the sources you use and the work you have done yourself. This proposed product is for learners who want a clear exercise, room to try things and a way to reflect on what they learned.

Libre AI Learning Session Facilitation

Prepare and follow a learning session with clear source material, participant roles and responsibilities. This proposed product is for trainers and facilitators who want to organize an activity and keep track of how the session unfolds.

Libre AI Personal Knowledge Notebook

Capture your notes, organize them and find useful information again while choosing what you share. This proposed product is for people building a personal collection of knowledge who want deliberate control over their notes and exports.

Libre AI Information Feed Filter

Turn information subscriptions into a selection you can inspect and understand. This proposed product is for people following several sources who want to decide what deserves their attention without relying on unexplained recommendations.

Libre AI Travel Itinerary Planner

Prepare a trip using destination information you can trace to its source. This proposed product is for travelers who want to see which activities could fit together and what still needs checking before relying on a plan.

Libre AI Public Vote Comparison

Compare positions you choose to state with sourced public voting records. This proposed product is for people examining public decisions who want to understand where their stated position agrees or differs.
